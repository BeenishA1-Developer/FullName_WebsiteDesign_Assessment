Create a professional, distinctive, high-fidelity UI/UX website design for a fictional Pakistani restaurant called "Mehran Desi Kitchen", located in Fateh Jang, Punjab, Pakistan.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:
This is a WEBSITE DESIGN ASSESSMENT for an internship. The design should demonstrate strong UI/UX fundamentals, visual design, responsive thinking, business understanding, content structure, and a consistent design system.

The restaurant serves traditional Pakistani food with a modern family-dining experience. Its menu includes Karahi, BBQ, Handi, Biryani, Pulao, Tikka, Kabab, Naan, Desserts, and Beverages.

BUSINESS PROBLEM:
The restaurant currently depends on walk-in customers, Facebook/Instagram, WhatsApp, and word of mouth. Customers need one professional place to discover the menu, understand the restaurant atmosphere, find the location, and contact or visit the restaurant.

PRIMARY WEBSITE GOAL:
Turn website visitors into customers by making it easy to:
1. View the menu
2. Find the location
3. Contact or visit the restaurant

DESIGN CONCEPT:
"From the Kitchen to the Table"

The website should communicate:
Local Pakistani Identity + Food Appeal + Trust + Convenience.

The visual experience should feel:
Modern, Premium, Warm, Authentic, Appetizing, and Family-Friendly.

Do NOT create a generic restaurant template. Build a distinctive visual identity inspired by Pakistani hospitality and traditional food culture, but keep it elegant, contemporary, usable, and professional. Avoid excessive cultural patterns, clutter, over-decoration, or stereotypical truck-art styling.

DESKTOP DESIGN:
Create a complete high-fidelity desktop homepage at 1440px width.

REQUIRED HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE:
1. Slim top information bar:
   - Fateh Jang, Punjab
   - Family Dining
   - Open Today

11. Final CTA:

12. Footer:

VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM:
Use a refined Pakistani-inspired palette:
- Deep Terracotta / Desi Red: #8F2D24
- Warm Ivory: #F8F4ED
- Charcoal Brown: #25201C
- Muted Brass: #B58A4A
- Deep Charcoal Text: #292522
- Warm Gray: #756E67

Typography direction:
- Elegant editorial serif for major headings, such as DM Serif Display
- Clean modern sans-serif for body text, such as Manrope
- Maintain excellent readability and hierarchy

DESIGN DETAILS:
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Generous spacing
- Consistent 12-column desktop grid
- Refined buttons with clear primary and secondary styles
- Subtle borders and controlled corner radius
- Minimal, tasteful decorative details inspired by Pakistani hospitality
- High-quality, consistent food photography
- Avoid excessive shadows, excessive rounded cards, neon colors, generic gradients, and overly futuristic AI-style visuals
- Make the website feel like a real premium local restaurant brand, not a template
