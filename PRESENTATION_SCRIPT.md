# Client Presentation Notes

## 1. Design Concept
The concept is **From the Kitchen to the Table**. Warm ivory, terracotta, charcoal, brass, editorial serif typography, and food-led imagery create a premium Pakistani family-dining experience without over-decoration.

## 2. Target Customer
Families, local Fateh Jang residents, young adults, groups, and travellers passing through Punjab who want trustworthy food, atmosphere, menu information, location, and easy contact from one homepage.

## 3. Key Design Decision
The menu stays within the homepage and uses category tabs with image-led item cards. This keeps the visitor in one clear journey while making the offering easy to scan.

## 4. Business Value
The website consolidates the restaurant's scattered discovery points into one conversion-focused experience. Visitors can understand the food, see the atmosphere, read reviews, view the menu, reserve, call, WhatsApp, or get directions without leaving the homepage.

## 5. Client Feedback Response
- Replace a purely plated-food impression with active cooking, BBQ, biryani, and interior imagery.
- Use warm brass accents, local hospitality language, and traditional editorial composition.
- Keep the cultural direction restrained so the result feels authentic and premium rather than decorative.

## Suggested 5-Minute Flow
1. Explain the client problem and the conversion goal.
2. Show the homepage journey: Discover -> Explore -> Trust -> Visit.
3. Show desktop composition and visual system.
4. Show mobile adaptation at 390px.
5. Demonstrate menu tabs, dish interaction, reservation form, and responsive behavior.
6. Close with business value and implementation readiness.
